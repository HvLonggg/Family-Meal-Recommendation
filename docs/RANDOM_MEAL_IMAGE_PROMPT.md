# Ảnh trái cây cho mâm cơm ngẫu nhiên

Chế độ tạo: công cụ ImageGen tích hợp (`imagegen`).

Prompt đã dùng:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural
> Asset type: square food photo for a Vietnamese meal-planning web app
> Primary request: A realistic Vietnamese family dessert plate of freshly cut seasonal tropical fruit, suitable as the “tráng miệng” dish in a home meal.
> Scene/backdrop: warm dark wooden Vietnamese dining table with a subtle woven rattan placemat.
> Subject: one round cream ceramic plate arranged naturally with ripe watermelon wedges, orange segments, dragon-fruit slices and small pieces of ripe mango; believable portions for four people.
> Style/medium: high-end but natural editorial food photography, true-to-life texture, small natural imperfections, not CGI.
> Composition/framing: square image, close three-quarter overhead angle, entire plate visible with safe crop space, no other dishes competing.
> Lighting/mood: soft daylight from a nearby window, warm welcoming family-meal atmosphere, gentle realistic shadows.
> Color palette: fresh red, orange, white-speckled dragon fruit and golden mango against cream ceramic and warm wood.
> Constraints: accurate real fruit, clean presentation, no text, no logo, no watermark, no people, no hands.
> Avoid: plastic-looking fruit, impossible cuts, duplicate fruit geometry, excessive garnish, restaurant branding, surreal utensils.
